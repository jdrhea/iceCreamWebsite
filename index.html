Ice Cream: Overview

Ice cream is a frozen treat typically made from dairy that has been flavoured with commonly sugar, and a flavor such as cocoa or vanilla, with fruit, such as strawberries or pecans. Food coloring is sometimes added in addition if it's American. The liquid is cooled below 32˚F/0˚C and stirred to incorporate air bubbles and prohibits the creation of ice crystals from forming. It can also be made by whisking a flavoured cream base and liquid nitrogen together. The result is a smooth foam that can only solidify at very low temperatures (below 2 °C or 35 °F). It becomes more malleable as its temperature increases.
